## drift_cyl

    python make_mesh.py cylinder --radius 10 --draft 10 --ntheta 32 --nz 8 --nr 6 --graded --out HullMesh.pnl

Cylinder a = 10 m, draft 10 m, depth 100 m, 448 panels, no external loads, rotation centre (0, 0, 0). `field_panels.txt` lists the hull panel whose centroid is each field point in `ControlFile.in`. The field points are written at full precision, computed from the node coordinates as written to the mesh, because the solver's on-body integrals are erratic for a point slightly off the panel.

Hydrostatics, from the 32-sided waterplane polygon, for a uniform solid: V = 3121.445 m3, Awp = 312.1445 m2, zG = zB = -5 m, M = 3.121445e6 kg, I44 = I55 = 1.815845e8 kg m2, I66 = 1.550726e8 kg m2, C33 = 3.061092e6 N/m, C44 = C55 = 7.603715e7 N m, and M15 = -M24 = M zG.

Natural frequencies, from a first-order sweep with the frequency-dependent added mass: heave 0.80 rad/s, pitch coupled with surge 0.76 rad/s. The frequencies straddle them without being at them. In a 0-degree wave, the motion per unit amplitude is about 1.8 m in heave and 0.2 rad in pitch at 0.70 rad/s, and 0.5 m in heave at 0.95 rad/s.

The irregular frequencies of the interior problem start at about 1.55 rad/s (J0(ka) = 0), above the frequencies used.

**The 1% limits of the radiated energy, Haskind and energy balance checks hold at these fixture frequencies only.** The case is a regression fixture, not a statement of accuracy over the frequency range. On this mesh, the energy balance is off by 1.3% at 0.65 rad/s, and the Haskind heave force by 1.3% at 1.20 rad/s and 2.2% at 1.30 rad/s.

On a uniform mesh refined from 384 to 3456 panels, the energy balance at 0.60 rad/s and the Haskind heave force at 1.30 rad/s converge at order 1.4 to 1.75 in panel size.

The cause of the Haskind heave error is not established. It grows smoothly with frequency (0.06%, 0.35%, 0.8%, 1.3%, 2.2% at 0.70, 0.95, 1.10, 1.20, 1.30 rad/s), while surge and pitch stay at or below about 0.18%. Two candidates remain: the panel size relative to the shorter wave, and the approach to the m = 0 irregular frequency near 1.55 rad/s.

The values in the last three paragraphs are from before the image term was integrated over the source panel. With it, the worst at the fixture frequencies are 0.77% for the radiated energy, 0.62% for the energy balance and 0.93% for the Haskind heave force.

**Rounded bilge: near-field converges to far-field on both kinds of rows.** At the finest mesh every entry on cosine rows is within 0.52%, the largest being near the heave resonance on the springs (about 0.86 rad/s). On uniform rows the largest are +1.25% and -0.58%, both at 0.70 rad/s. Neither kind is closer everywhere: cosine rows are closer at 0.70 rad/s and uniform rows on the moving hull at 1.10 rad/s and on the springs at 0.95 rad/s. The top wall row of the cosine meshes has height over width 0.09, 0.045, 0.031 at 480, 1920, 4320 panels, so these are the aspect ratios this result covers. All the fixtures with this hull use cosine rows.

The terms of F_x agree between the two at 1.10 rad/s on the springs, at 480, 1920, 4320 panels:

Before the solver integrated the free-surface image term over the source panel, cosine rows did not converge here: on the springs at 1.10 rad/s the error grew as +3.8%, +4.9%, +6.1% at the same meshes, with term II at -2.76, -2.77, -2.74. See `docs/developer_documentation.md` for that change. There is no run-time warning for thin hull rows, because this measurement leaves no error on them to warn about.

**Sharp bilge: the error falls slowly, and is a separate error.** The image term hardly moved it: free at 0.95 rad/s and 4032 panels was +21.5% before and is +21.3% now. Term II on the wall rows nearest the bilge holds the excess. An offline fit of the edge behaviour there, made before the image term change and with an earlier neighbour rule, brought it to +0.9% fixed and +5.7% free at 4032 panels, 0.95 rad/s. CI pins these hulls with a regression band, `*_sharp_regression`, which holds near-field and far-field each within 1% of the recorded values. It is not an accuracy claim. A 5% limit is run outside CI by `near_far_study.py`, and it is missed there.

**CI on the rounded bilge.** `fixed_near_far`, `free_near_far`, `external_stiffness` and `two_body_total` hold 5% on cosine rows. `drift_round_kstf` at 0.95 rad/s has a band only, near its resonance: at 480 panels F_x is +4.4% at heading 0 and +7.3% at heading 30, where the force vector is 2.7% off.

**At 0.70 rad/s,** the free tests print the difference and do not check it. The net force is 4.6% of term I on the rounded hull, the difference of terms much larger than it. The difference falls with refinement on both kinds of rows, as the table shows.
