# Damping lid fixtures

Used by `test/test_integration_lid.f90`. Generated by `pre_postprocessing_python/DampingLid/make_lid_fixtures.py` and committed as they are. Every case copies the hull, hydrostatics and control file of `test/data/image_term/image_cos` (cylinder of radius 5 m and draft 5 m, 180 panels, 50 m of water, 1.35 rad/s, heading 0), adds `Damping_lid 1` after `Number of threads`, and writes `DampingLid.pnl` from `annulus(r0, r1, nt, nr)`, which gives nt x nr panels between radius r0 and r1 on z = 0, or a disc when r0 = 0.

| Case | Lid | Change | Expected |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `lid_stop_missing` | none | no `DampingLid.pnl` | stops, `DampingLid.pnl input file missing` |
| `lid_stop_offplane` | r 6 to 10, 20 x 2 | node 5 at z = 1e-3 | stops, `lid node is not on z = 0` |
| `lid_stop_symmetry` | r 6 to 10, 20 x 2 | lid header `ISY = 1`, hull 0 | stops, `lid symmetry differs from the hull` |
| `lid_stop_gmres` | r 6 to 10, 20 x 2 | `Solver_type 2` | stops, `damping lid needs Solver_type 1` |
| `lid_stop_inside` | disc r 0 to 2.5, 12 x 2 | inside the hull | stops, `lid panel inside a hull` |
| `lid_flag_off` | r 6 to 10, 20 x 2 | `Damping_lid 0` | `AmssDamp.1` and `ExcForce.3` equal those of `image_cos` |

Solid angle of the hull and its image at the lid centroids, measured while building the check: 12.566 (4 pi) at every centroid of `lid_stop_inside`, and below 1.3e-14 in magnitude at every centroid of the annulus r 6 to 10. The same on the half hull (`ISY = 1`) and on two bodies 5 km apart.
