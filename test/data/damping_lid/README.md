- R stays bounded as rho falls to 0.001 H at all three frequencies, so the log coefficients of G_finite and G_deep match, and the remainder is a smooth term.
- The jump in R between rho/H = 0.01 and 0.03 is 2e-5, 2.5e-4 and 1.1e-3 at nu = 0.05, 0.19 and 1, which is 1e-5, 1.2e-4 and 3e-4 of abs(G) at 0.03 H: below the 1% expected.

The series reference overflows at the same place, through cosh(kh). So `finite_tends_to_deep` checks H = 300 instead of the planned 5000.

## Field output with a lid

`CalPotential` (`src/PressureElevation.f90`) adds the lid's single layer when `LIDON = 1`: `4 pi phi(p) = sum_hull (S V - D phi) + sum_lid S q`, the sum of `S(p, l) q_l` over the lid panels and symmetry images (the field point reflected as in the hull loop), before the division by the free term. The free term stays 4 pi for field points on z = 0 in the fluid. With the lid off nothing changes. With the lid on, the radiation rows of points on z = 0 (elevation) are also written to `PressureElevation.6p`; they were not before.

The sign of q is not seen in A, B or X. It rests on this derivation, whose rows are hull `2 pi phi + D phi - S q = S V` and lid `D phi - S q = S V` (+ `4 pi phi_I` in diffraction), and on the test `lid_elevation_zero_at_centroids`: with the opposite sign the elevation at a lid centroid would be about twice the incident one, not about zero.

Normalisation: the elevation of `PressureElevation.6p` is the WAMIT non-dimensional one, divided by the incident amplitude, so the incident wave has amplitude 1 and the diffraction values are in units of it. Radiation values are per unit motion, so they are compared with the largest radiation value at points off the lid.

New cases: `lid_hull_160` is `lid_ann_160` with the 180 hull centroids of `image_cos` as field points. `lid_field_off_centroid` is `lid_ann_160` with field points at the lid node (7, 0, 0), the middle of a radial edge (7.5, 0, 0), a point inside the first panel off its centroid, the 10 first centroids, (0, 8, 0) (a node of the radius 8 ring) and (20, 0, 0). `lid_ann_640` has two more field points, at (12, 0, 0) and (20, 0, 0), for the scale.

## Several bodies

`src/DampingLidMulti.f90` solves the same equations as the single-body solve, on the combined hull arrays of all bodies (`*_MULTI_COMB`): hull rows from `FILL_HULL_LEFT_MULTI` (`src/AssbMatxMulti.f90`, the fill of `ASSB_LEFT_MULTI` moved out unchanged) and `ASSB_RBC_MULTI` over the panels of each body, lid rows from `HULL_LAYERS_MULTI` and `LID_SINGLE_LAYER`. Mode m of body k is column 6(k - 1) + m of `MXPOT_MULTI_COMB` and `MXQ`, diffraction column 6 NBODY + 1. `CalPotentialMulti` adds the lid term as `CalPotential` does. The lid is read and checked once, after the combined arrays are built. Its `ISX` and `ISY` are compared with those of the last hull read. The multi-body path takes `NSYS` from the last hull as well, so a run whose bodies differ in symmetry is wrong with or without a lid, and the comparison is right whenever the bodies agree.

### IRSP with several bodies: not used

Read in `src/HAMS_Prog.f90` (multi-body branch) and `src/AssbMatx_irrMulti.f90`: with `IRSP = 1` the multi-body path puts the hull rows 1 to `NELEM_TOTAL` and the waterplane rows after them in one matrix of `TNELEM` rows, `TNELEM` counting the waterplane panels too, and solves the normal equations. Comments in `HAMS_Prog.f90` say that irregular frequency removal is implemented for single bodies only, and no committed test runs it with several bodies. The lid path cannot reuse it unchanged. `ASSB_RBC_MULTI`, which gives the hull right-hand sides of the lid solve, loops over `TNELEM` rows, while `RKBN_MULTI_COMB` has `NELEM_TOTAL` rows when `IRSP = 1`. The single-body approach (interior rows at the waterplane centroids, least squares) would need a new hull right-hand side and the combined waterplane arrays as row points. So a lid with several bodies and `If_remove_irr_freq 1` stops (`lid_stop_irsp_two`), with the message `damping lid with several bodies needs If_remove_irr_freq 0`.
