# Damping lid fixtures

Used by `test/test_integration_lid.f90`. Generated by `pre_postprocessing_python/DampingLid/make_lid_fixtures.py` and committed as they are. Every `DampingLid.pnl` has the damping block, one damper over all panels with epsilon 0.2 m/s unless the case says otherwise. Every single-body case copies the hull, hydrostatics and control file of `test/data/image_term/image_cos` (cylinder of radius 5 m and draft 5 m, 180 panels, 50 m of water, 1.35 rad/s, heading 0), adds `Damping_lid 1` after `Number of threads`, and writes `DampingLid.pnl` from `annulus(r0, r1, nt, nr)`, which gives nt x nr panels between radius r0 and r1 on z = 0, or a disc when r0 = 0. The two-body cases are in [Several bodies](#several-bodies).

Derived from these tables:

- R stays bounded as rho falls to 0.001 H at all three frequencies, so the log coefficients of G_finite and G_deep match, and the remainder is a smooth term.
- The jump in R between rho/H = 0.01 and 0.03 is 2e-5, 2.5e-4 and 1.1e-3 at nu = 0.05, 0.19 and 1, which is 1e-5, 1.2e-4 and 3e-4 of abs(G) at 0.03 H: below the 1% expected.
- From rho/H = 0.05 up `FINGREEN3D` matches the series to 3e-10 or better (2.4e-10 at nu = 0.05). Between 0.0005 and 0.05 (`PIDCOCK`) it is off by a near constant 7.9e-6, and below 0.003 H it scatters, to 1.1e-2 at 0.001 H and nu = 1.
- At nu = 1 and kH = 50 the bottom adds only 7.6e-6 to G. Most of the measured R, up to 3.7e-3, is the error of the deep-water approximation in `INFGREEN3D` (`HavelockGF`), about 1e-3 of abs(G); adding R corrects it. That error varies on the scale 1/nu, not H, so sampling R at 0.03 H rather than at the true distance leaves an error of the same order, 2.4e-3 at nu = 1 between nu*rho = 0.3 and 1.5, or 2e-4 of abs(G).
- `FINGREEN3D` returns NaN from kh = 360 (measured with a unit self panel at nu = 1: 2.4e-5 from deep water at H = 300, 8.1e-6 at H = 340, NaN at 360, 400 and 5000). The series reference overflows at the same place, through cosh(kh). So `finite_tends_to_deep` checks H = 300 instead of the planned 5000.

Other measurements, from the test output: the Haskind heave residual is 2.07e-3 with no lid and with epsilon 0, and 4.6e-2 with epsilon 0.2. The lid power at the 160 centroids of `lid_ann_160`, 1/2 rho omega**2 sum_l epsilon_l abs(eta_l)**2 A_l with the one-point rule per panel, is 4.84e4 W per m**2 of incident amplitude in diffraction (lid area 200.24 m**2), and in radiation 4.8% (surge) and 4.5% (heave) of the power the body radiates, 1/2 omega**2 B_jj per unit motion. Derived from these:

- epsilon = 0 gives alpha = 0, and the lid rows then only evaluate the representation: A, B, X and the field values of `lid_ann_160_eps0` equal those of `lid_nolid_160` to the last printed digit (measured 0). This also checks that the lid columns carry no term other than -alpha S, and that the incident lid term vanishes with alpha.
- Two dampers of equal epsilon give the results of one, to the last printed digit (measured 0); with 1.0 m/s on half the lid B11 changes by 7.9%. So the matrix, the incident term and the field use the epsilon of each panel.
- Sign of the lid. With epsilon > 0, Im(alpha) > 0 and the lid power is >= 0 by its formula. Not decisive on its own, so `lid_absorbs` also checks that the lid power in radiation is a part of the radiated power (ratio 4.8%, below 1). Derived, not measured: B_jj = far-field power + lid power, both >= 0. B11 falls from 206.25 (no lid) to 199.71 (epsilon 0.2): a fall is allowed, since the lid can cut the far-field radiation by more than it absorbs. The rigid lid (epsilon 1e6, lid power about 0) cuts B11 to 48.9.
- At epsilon 0.2 the lid is weak at this frequency: omega epsilon / g = 0.028, so alpha / nu is about 0.028 i, and the results move by 3% from no lid. The rigid lid moves them by up to a factor of 4.
- The Haskind relation over the hull does not hold with epsilon > 0, since the lid absorbs (spec): 4.6e-2 at 0.2 against 2.1e-3 with no lid.
- Convergence from 40 to 160 to 640 lid panels: the change falls by 3.1 (A11), 4.0 (A33), 8.5 (B11), 4.3 (B33) and 8.6 (abs(X1)). B33 converges now; with the phi = 0 lid it did not at 40 panels and was not checked.

- The A and B asymmetry, 6.1e-3 at most, is the hull's own (`image_cos`: 2.1e-3 in A, 6.1e-3 in B).

## Field output with a lid

`CalPotential` (`src/PressureElevation.f90`) adds the lid term when `LIDON = 1`: `4 pi phi(p) = sum_hull (S V - D phi) + sum_lid alpha_l S (phi_l + phi_I,l)`, phi_I,l the incident potential at the lid centroid in diffraction only (the lid unknown of diffraction is the scattered potential). The sum runs over the lid panels and symmetry images (the field point reflected as in the hull loop, the incident potential at the reflected centroid). Panels with alpha = 0 add nothing. The free term stays 4 pi for field points on z = 0 in the fluid. `CalElevation` gives eta = i omega phi / (g - i omega epsilon) at a point on z = 0 in or on the edge of a lid panel of epsilon > 0, found by `LID_EPS_AT` for the point and its symmetry images, and i omega phi / g elsewhere and for epsilon = 0. With the lid off nothing changes. With the lid on, the radiation rows of points on z = 0 (elevation) are also written to `PressureElevation.6p`.

These replace the phi = 0 field output, whose test `lid_elevation_zero_at_centroids` (elevation 7.0e-8 at the centroids) is removed: with epsilon > 0 the elevation on the lid is not zero.

Normalisation: the elevation of `PressureElevation.6p` is the WAMIT non-dimensional one, divided by the incident amplitude, so the incident wave has amplitude 1. Radiation values are per unit motion.

Cases: `lid_ann_160` and its variants (`_eps0`, `_rigid`, `_two_dampers`, `_split`) and `lid_nolid_160` have field points at all 160 lid centroids, then (12, 0, 0), (20, 0, 0) and (12, 0, -3). `lid_hull_160` is `lid_ann_160` with the 180 hull centroids of `image_cos`. `lid_field_off_centroid` is `lid_ann_160` with field points at the lid node (7, 0, 0), the middle of a radial edge (7.5, 0, 0), a point inside the first panel off its centroid, the 10 first centroids, (0, 8, 0) (a node of the radius 8 ring) and (20, 0, 0).

## Several bodies

`src/DampingLidMulti.f90` solves the same equations as the single-body solve, on the combined hull arrays of all bodies (`*_MULTI_COMB`): hull rows from `FILL_HULL_LEFT_MULTI` (`src/AssbMatxMulti.f90`, the fill of `ASSB_LEFT_MULTI` moved out unchanged) and `ASSB_RBC_MULTI` over the panels of each body, lid rows from `HULL_LAYERS_MULTI` and `LID_SINGLE_LAYER`. Mode m of body k is column 6(k - 1) + m of `MXPOT_MULTI_COMB` and `MXLPOT` (the lid phi), diffraction column 6 NBODY + 1. The epsilon lid of the single body is used as it is: the lid columns -alpha_l S (kept before scaling in `LCOLM`), 4 pi on the lid diagonal, the incident lid term on every row in diffraction, and the lid elevation in `CalElevationMulti`. `CalPotentialMulti` adds the lid term as `CalPotential` does. The lid is read and checked once, after the combined arrays are built. Its `ISX` and `ISY` are compared with those of the last hull read. The multi-body path takes `NSYS` from the last hull as well, so a run whose bodies differ in symmetry is wrong with or without a lid, and the comparison is right whenever the bodies agree.

### IRSP with several bodies: not used

Read in `src/HAMS_Prog.f90` (multi-body branch) and `src/AssbMatx_irrMulti.f90`: with `IRSP = 1` the multi-body path puts the hull rows 1 to `NELEM_TOTAL` and the waterplane rows after them in one matrix of `TNELEM` rows, `TNELEM` counting the waterplane panels too, and solves the normal equations. Comments in `HAMS_Prog.f90` say that irregular frequency removal is implemented for single bodies only, and no committed test runs it with several bodies. The lid path cannot reuse it unchanged. `ASSB_RBC_MULTI`, which gives the hull right-hand sides of the lid solve, loops over `TNELEM` rows, while `RKBN_MULTI_COMB` has `NELEM_TOTAL` rows when `IRSP = 1`. The single-body approach (interior rows at the waterplane centroids, least squares) would need a new hull right-hand side and the combined waterplane arrays as row points. So a lid with several bodies and `If_remove_irr_freq 1` stops (`lid_stop_irsp_two`), with the message `damping lid with several bodies needs If_remove_irr_freq 0`.
